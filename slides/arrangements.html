Three ways to gather

Quiet shelf
A broad scan of many learning traces. Designed for identifying recurring patterns and high-level observations across the entire commons.
Study table
A smaller, calmer grouping. Ideal for comparing a handful of related notes and methods in a semi-focused environment.
Loose notes
One focused trace at a time. Bounded navigation with reading-time context to ensure deep engagement with a single idea.
All arrangements preserve content-only public metadata.
